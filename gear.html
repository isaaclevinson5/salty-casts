Recommended Shore Spinning Setups

A versatile shore-spinning setup needs enough length to cast over breaking waves and enough backbone to steer fish away from jagged rocks. Use the comparison table below to match your setup to your target conditions.

Setup Tier Rod Length & Action Lure Weight Rating Reel Size Mainline & Leader
Light Estuary / Harbor 8'0" – 8'6" Fast 5g – 20g 2500 – 3000 0.10mm PE Braid + 0.22mm Fluoro
All-Round Coastal (Best Starter) 9'0" – 9'6" Fast 10g – 35g 3000 – 4000 0.12mm–0.14mm 8X PE + 0.26mm–0.30mm Fluoro
Heavy Surf & Wind 9'6" – 10'0" Med-Heavy 15g – 50g 4000 – 5000 0.16mm 8X PE + 0.35mm–0.40mm Fluoro

Step-by-Step: Tying Your Braid-to-Leader Knot

Connecting thin braided mainline directly to a 1-meter fluorocarbon shock leader prevents abrasion on rocks and makes your lure presentation nearly invisible. Follow these steps or watch the demonstration video below:

  1. Overlap about 15 cm of your 8X braided mainline and your fluorocarbon leader.
  2. Wrap the braid tightly around the leader 10–12 times going up, then 8–10 wraps back down.
  3. Pass the tag end through the loop, moisten the line with water, and pull both mainlines tight to cinch the coils.
  4. Finish with two half-hitches and trim the tag ends close so the knot glides smoothly through your rod guides.
