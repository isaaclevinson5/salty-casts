Essential Coastal Lure Arsenal

Matching your lure weight and swimming depth to the wind and water clarity is the key to consistent hookups. Below are four staple lure styles every shore angler should carry:

Lure Type Ideal Size & Weight Best Conditions Primary Target Species
Slim Suspending Jerkbait 130mm – 150mm / 15g – 22g Calm mornings, dusk, clear harbor water Barracuda (Bicuda), Sea Bass (Lubina)
Heavy Sinking Minnow 110mm – 140mm / 26g – 40g Strong headwinds, rough surf, deep drop-offs Sea Bass, Bluefish, Amberjack
Metal Casting Jig / Spoon 65mm – 80mm / 20g – 40g Maximum casting distance, schooling baitfish Mackerel, Bonito, Lizardfish, Barracuda
Soft Plastic Swimbait 90mm – 120mm / 10g – 20g jighead Whitewater foam, rocky reefs, sandy troughs Sea Bass (Lubina), Flounder

Lure Retrieval & Twitching Technique

Casting out and reeling straight back only works half the time. Predators like barracuda and sea bass often strike during sudden changes in speed or pauses:

  • Jerk-Jerk-Pause (Minnows): Give the rod tip two sharp downward twitches, reel in the slack, and pause for 1–2 seconds so the lure suspends like an injured sardine.
  • Slow Steady Roll (Soft Plastics): Keep the rod tip at a 45-degree angle and maintain a slow, steady retrieve just above the bottom rocks.
