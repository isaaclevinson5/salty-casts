Welcome to Salty Casts

Salty Casts is your practical guide to shore and surf fishing. Whether you are casting from sandy beaches or rocky points, learn how to read the tides, pick the right lures, and land more fish from the shore.

What You Will Find Here

  • Top coastal fishing locations and access tips
  • Lure and tackle recommendations for seabass, barracuda, and sea bream
  • Basic surf safety, tide reading, and local catch reports
