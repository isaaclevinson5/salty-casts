Quick-Start Shore Fishing Checklist

  • Valid Recreational Sea Fishing License: Always carry your local coastal fishing permit before making your first cast.
  • Polarized Sunglasses: Cuts surface glare so you can spot submerged rocks, drop-offs, and following fish.
  • Split-Ring Pliers & Fish Gripper: Essential for safely unhooking sharp-toothed predators like barracuda.
  • Freshwater Rinse Routine: Rinse your reel, rod guides, and treble hooks with fresh water after every saltwater session to prevent corrosion.
